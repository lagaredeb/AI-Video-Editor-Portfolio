How it works

  1. Add, edit or remove videos below. Your changes are kept in this browser until you publish.
  2. Click Download videos.json.
  3. Replace the old videos.json in your Portfolio folder with the downloaded one.
  4. Upload it to your website (VS Code: Commit, then Sync Changes; Netlify: drag the folder again).

Visitors who open this page can't change your site. Only the file you upload goes live.
